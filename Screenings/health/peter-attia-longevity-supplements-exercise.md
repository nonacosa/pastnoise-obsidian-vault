# Rehearsing the Last Decade

> Peter Attia's longevity exercise is less a fountain than a practice of keeping capability honest.

## Core Thesis
Peter Attia's longevity project, heard through Ferriss's cave, a Google talk, and a fitness podcast, treats aging as a problem of capability and risk rather than vanity. Exercise is the practical spine: a repeated stress that rehearses the demands of late life. Yet the dossier also holds friction—early mentorship, accidental death, and critics fact-checking popular longevity books—so the worldview is neither fountain nor formula. It is disciplined uncertainty: train the body, interrogate the evidence, and keep the promise honest.

## 30-Second Takeaways
- Attia's longevity medicine treats exercise as a rehearsal for late-life capability, not a cosmetic add-on or a guarantee of thinness.
- The cave conversation with Ferriss frames longevity as intimate, experimental work conducted in ordinary rooms with ordinary habits repeated over decades.
- Attia's early teacher and the Google stage show that scientific identity can begin with mentorship, correction, and a timely invitation to think.
- FoundMyFitness widens the frame beyond chronic disease: accidental death reminds us that longevity is also contingency, judgment, and risk management.
- Viva Longevity supplies necessary friction, insisting that popular books about aging can inspire while still failing when checked against evidence.
