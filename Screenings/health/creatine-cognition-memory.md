# The Quiet Fuel of Memory

> Creatine, cognition, and the metabolic grace of a mind that can still remember.

**Watch with synced bilingual subtitles**: [English Screening Room](https://pastnoise.com/film/creatine-cognition-memory) | [中文双语放映室](https://pastnoise.com/zh/film/creatine-cognition-memory)

## Core Thesis
Creatine is not merely a muscle supplement; it is a metabolic substrate for the brain's most demanding work. Across the documentary acts, researchers and clinicians converge on a quiet truth: creatine supports cognition, especially when energy demands spike—during sleep deprivation, aging, or the slow erosion of memory. Yet the evidence is nuanced, dose-dependent, and often more promising in older adults than the young. The written record confirms this, while the spoken excerpts reveal a deeper human story: memory is not just recall, but the vitality of a life still fully lived.

## 30-Second Takeaways
- Creatine supplementation appears to enhance cognition particularly in older adults, with effects on memory and processing speed.
- High-dose creatine protocols are being explored for Alzheimer's disease, targeting brain creatine levels and cognitive function.
- Sleep deprivation creates metabolic dysfunction that creatine may partially offset, suggesting a role in cognitive resilience.
- Creatine increases brain energy and may improve low mood, linking cellular metabolism to emotional and cognitive health.
- Grandmothers who regularly care for grandchildren show slower cognitive decline, hinting that engaged living itself is neuroprotective.
