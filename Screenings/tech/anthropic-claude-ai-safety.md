## Core Thesis
Anthropic's rise is not merely a story of bigger models but of embedding safety into the very act of scaling. From prompt engineers who put safety first to partnerships that accelerate Claude without abandoning caution, the company treats responsibility as an engineering discipline, not a marketing slogan. The result is a rare alignment of ethics and performance—where fewer tool calls and lower token consumption coexist with a public benefit charter. This dossier explores how that balance is built, tested, and sometimes strained.

## 30-Second Takeaways
- Safety at Anthropic is not a brake on scaling but a design principle that shapes prompts, policies, and partnerships from the ground up.
- Efficiency gains in models like Fable 5—fewer tool calls, lower token use—demonstrate that responsible engineering can also be lean engineering.
- The Responsible Scaling Policy provides a formal framework, yet its real test lies in daily decisions by prompt engineers and product teams.
- Partnerships with Microsoft and NVIDIA signal that safety-focused AI is becoming infrastructure, not niche—accelerating Claude's reach while raising stakes.
- The tension between open commentary and controlled messaging reveals how Anthropic manages public perception of its safety-first identity.
